What it does

Everything OpenCode does, plus a few touches of our own.

Terminal first

A friendly console interface: mouse, scrolling and shortcuts work out of the box.

Desktop app

A ready-to-use app for Windows: tabs, sessions and the theme you like.

Model providers

Works with any AI model: one running on your PC or in the cloud. Your choice.

Agents and plugins

Add your own tools and scripts. Shape it around your workflow.

Its own mascot

A shark-cat holding an OK sign: splash screen, window title, and the About section.

"OK" Pet

A plush cat on top of the app: drag and toss him, and he rolls, bounces, and falls asleep. Double-click for a meow and hearts.

Smooth transitions

Tabs and screens fade in softly.

Install

Download the ready build and run it - no building from source needed.

Linux: AppImage · DEB

The installer is unsigned: SmartScreen may warn. Click "More info" then "Run anyway".

The latest.yml and OpenOK-win-x64.exe.blockmap files are needed for auto-update - without latest.yml the app will not learn about the new version.

About

OpenOK is a fork of OpenCode. We do not touch the core without a reason: the goal is careful visual changes, our own icons, and joy in the terminal. All code is open under MIT.
